What this is

AeroMedQBank provides practice questions, explanations and short study lessons for residents studying for aerospace, occupational and preventive medicine board exams. It is an unofficial study aid run by an individual. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by the U.S. Army, the Department of Defense, the American Board of Preventive Medicine, or any other board or agency. Names of exams, boards and organizations belong to their owners and are used only to say what the app helps you prepare for.

Education only, not medical advice

  • The questions and lessons are for exam preparation. Do not use it to make decisions about patients or aircrew, and do not treat it as regulation, policy or clinical guidance. Always use current official sources.
  • Questions may contain mistakes or be out of date. New questions are reviewed before members can see them, but a review does not make a question error-free. Please use the Feedback button to report problems.
  • Using the app does not guarantee any exam result.
  • Labels such as "High yield" are the developer's judgment about what is worth studying first. They are not official exam content, not a prediction of what will be on any exam, and not a reason to skip the rest of the material.
